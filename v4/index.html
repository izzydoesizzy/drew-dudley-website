The Speaker

On a mission to
redefine leadership.

With more than five million views, his TED talk "Everyday Leadership (The Lollipop Moment)" proved that leadership is not a characteristic of an elite few, but one that lives within us all.

Drew is the founder and Chief Catalyst of Day One Leadership, where he has helped organizations around the world increase their leadership capacity. His clients have included Coca-Cola, American Express, JP Morgan Chase, the United Nations, and over 100 colleges and universities. Through his high-energy talks, he shares practical, actionable insights on building cultures of leadership that drive pride, productivity, happiness, and retention.

Before founding Day One, Drew spent eight years directing one of Canada's largest leadership development programs at the University of Toronto, and chaired Shinerama, Canada's largest student-run fundraiser, mobilizing 35,000 volunteers to raise over $1M a year for Cystic Fibrosis Canada. His first book, This Is Day One, debuted at #6 on the Wall Street Journal bestseller list.

Signature Keynotes

Six talks. One belief:
leadership lives in everyone.

Every keynote is tailored to the room. Tap any title to read more.

01

The Leadership Test: Six Questions That Transform People & Organizations

Fewer than 1% of audience members will call themselves a leader. Drew demonstrates a leadership driven by six values, impact, courage, growth, empowerment, class, and self-respect, and gives the room six simple questions that change behavior, careers, and organizations.

+
02

This Is Day One: Creating Cultures of Leadership

Leadership is a daily choice, not a title. Treating every day as Day One strengthens commitment to core values. People who get clear on those values rate up to 140% higher with the teams they lead. Drew shows audiences how to turn values into daily behavior.

+
03

Courage: Leadership Impact in Action

Why positive change gets delayed by the fear of loss, and how to act anyway. Audiences learn the difference between confidence and courage, how to prepare for failure, and why failure is a strategy, not just a lesson.

+
04

Self Respect: The Foundation of Leadership

"There is no hole in your life that cannot be filled with self-respect." Drew weaves self-care with his own story, sobriety, mental health, and a 100-pound transformation, to show how every decision either builds or erodes the respect leadership requires.

+
05

Elevate Don't Escalate: Leading With Class

"More and more, we've come to expect less and less from one another." Most dysfunction comes from fear. Drew gives teams practical tools to build psychologically safe workplaces and to elevate, instead of escalate, in the moments that matter.

+
06

The Game Has No Winners: Leading Beyond Scarcity

Scarcity thinking, the race for limited applause and acclaim, breeds dysfunction. Drew offers a roadmap to an economy of abundance through growth and empowerment, including the two most important words in leadership.
