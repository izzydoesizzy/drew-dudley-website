The Content

The Roadmap
to Impact.

Six keynotes built on one idea: leadership is a series of moments of impact. Book one, or combine them into a program built for your room.

Foundational

01

The Leadership Test

Six Questions That Transform People & Organizations

Drew has asked thousands of audiences one question, and fewer than 1% will call themselves a leader. This keynote hands them six daily questions, across impact, courage, growth, empowerment, class, and self-respect, that turn leadership from a title into a habit.

"How many of you are completely comfortable calling yourself a leader?"

02

This Is Day One

Creating Cultures of Leadership

Leadership reframed as a daily choice, not a set of titles earned over time. People who get clear on their values rate up to 140% higher with the teams they lead, and Drew shows audiences how to turn those values into everyday behavior.

"Good leaders live their values whenever they get the chance. Great leaders create opportunities to live their values."

Values-Based

03

Courage

Leadership Impact in Action

Most positive change gets delayed by the fear of loss. Drew shares practical strategies for acting anyway: the difference between confidence and courage, and why failure is a strategy rather than a verdict.

"No one's interested in something you didn't do." · Gord Downie

04

Self-Respect

The Foundation of Leadership

His most personal talk. Drew weaves self-care insights with his own story, sobriety, mental health, a 100-pound transformation, to show that we cannot lead others until we can lead ourselves.

"There is no hole in your life that cannot be filled with self-respect."

05

"The Game" Has No Winners

Growth, Empowerment & Leading Beyond Scarcity

We are taught to compete for scarce resources, and it quietly breaks our teams. Drew offers a roadmap to an economy of abundance built on growth and empowerment, including the two most important words in leadership.

"Don't start chasing applause and acclaim. That way lies madness." · Ron Swanson

06

Elevate Don't Escalate

Leading with Impact & Living with Class

More and more, we expect less and less from one another. Drew gives teams the tools to build psychologically safe cultures and to elevate, instead of escalate, in the moments that test us.

"More and more we've come to expect less and less from one another, and I think that should change." · Aaron Sorkin
