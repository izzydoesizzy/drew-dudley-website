01

The Leadership Test

Fewer than 1% of people will call themselves a leader. Drew's six-question Leadership Test, built on impact, courage, growth, empowerment, class, and self-respect, changes that, and the behavior that follows.

02

This Is Day One

Leadership is a daily choice, not a title. Treating every day as Day One, audiences learn to define their core values and turn them into the behaviors that build cultures of leadership.

03

Courage: Leadership in Action

Why we delay the changes that matter, and how to act anyway. The difference between confidence and courage, and how to treat failure as a strategy rather than a verdict.

04

Elevate Don't Escalate

"More and more, we've come to expect less and less from one another." Drew gives teams the tools to build psychologically safe, high-class cultures, and to elevate instead of escalate.
