The Idea

Leadership isn't about titles. It's about choosing impact, every single day.

It's the moment you said something to someone, maybe years ago, that changed the course of their life. You probably don't even remember it. Drew has spent two decades teaching people to notice those moments, and to create more of them on purpose.

Signature Keynotes

Pick the room.
He'll build the talk.

01The Leadership Test+

Fewer than 1% of people will call themselves a leader. Drew's six-question Leadership Test, built on impact, courage, growth, empowerment, class, and self-respect, changes that, and the behavior that follows it.

02This Is Day One+

Leadership is a daily choice, not a title. Treating every day as Day One, audiences define their core values and turn them into the behaviors that build cultures of leadership.

03Courage: Leadership in Action+

Why we delay the changes that matter, and how to act anyway. The difference between confidence and courage, and how to treat failure as a strategy rather than a verdict.

04Elevate Don't Escalate+

"More and more, we've come to expect less and less from one another." The tools to build psychologically safe, high-class cultures, and to elevate instead of escalate.
