Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This is a starting template. Have it reviewed against the laws that apply to you (for example PIPEDA in Canada, GDPR in the EU) before you rely on it.

This policy explains what information Day One Leadership and Drew Dudley collect when you visit this site or get in touch, and how it is used. By using the site or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We only collect what you choose to share. When you submit a booking inquiry, through the form or by email, that typically includes:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your organization
  • Event details such as date, location, format, audience size, and budget range
  • Anything else you include in your message

The site may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics (such as pages viewed) to help us improve it.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, arrange a speaking engagement, and communicate with you about your event. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Sharing

Your details are shared only with Drew and the Day One Leadership team as needed to respond to you and deliver your event. We may use trusted service providers (for example email and scheduling tools) who process information on our behalf.

Third-party content

This site embeds a video from Vimeo and links to external pages such as TED and Amazon. Those services have their own privacy policies, which govern any information they collect.

Retention

We keep inquiry information for as long as needed to respond and to maintain reasonable business records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have shared at any time. Just email drew@dayoneleadership.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at drew@dayoneleadership.com or 1.855.685.3253.
